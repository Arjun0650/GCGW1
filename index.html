02 / WHAT WE DO

Specialised, practical solutions.

Every project begins with a clear understanding of the structure, the cause of deterioration and the right method of repair. We coordinate each stage with attention to durability, site safety and dependable execution.

01

Guniting

Controlled sprayed-concrete application for strengthening, profiling and concrete surface rehabilitation.

02

Structural repairs

Targeted repair of damaged, cracked or deteriorated structural elements after a practical on-site assessment.

03

Concrete rehabilitation

Repair and protection measures to address deterioration and help extend the useful life of concrete structures.

04

Grouting & crack treatment

Purpose-led treatments for voids, seepage paths and cracks, selected to suit the condition on site.

05

Specialised civil works

Reliable execution for complex civil-work requirements, coordinated around real site constraints.
