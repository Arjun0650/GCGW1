01 / About GCGW

Repair is a method, not a patch.

Deterioration in concrete rarely has a single cause. GCGW starts with the structure itself — how it is built, how it is used and what the damage is doing — before proposing guniting, rehabilitation or civil repair work.

Based in Yousufguda, Hyderabad, we work on housing society buildings, industrial structures, commercial buildings and slope protection across six states. The scope is agreed in writing, the sequence is planned around access and safety, and the work is carried out to the approved specification.

  • Site-specific assessment before a method is proposed
  • Written scope and method statement
  • Material, access and safety planning agreed in advance
  • Finishing and curing treated as part of the work

03 / How we work

Five steps from inspection to handover.

The same sequence applies whether the work is a single retaining wall or a multi-floor rehabilitation programme.

  1. Step 01

    Site inspection

    We visit the site, review access and look at the visible condition of the structure.

  2. Step 02

    Assessment

    We consider the likely cause and extent of the deterioration, and review drawings or specifications you can share.

  3. Step 03

    Method statement

    We set out the repair scope, materials, sequence and safety measures before work starts.

  4. Step 04

    Execution

    Surface preparation, mesh fixing, guniting or other repair work is carried out to the agreed specification, followed by finishing and curing.

  5. Step 05

    Handover

    We review the finished work with you and set out any follow-up checks.

07 / Questions

Answers before you call.

If your question is not here, send it through the enquiry form or call the primary number.

What is guniting?

Guniting is mortar or concrete sprayed at high velocity onto a prepared surface. It is used to strengthen, re-profile or rebuild damaged concrete on walls, columns, beams and slopes.

Is guniting the same as shotcrete?

They describe the same sprayed-concrete process. Guniting usually refers to the dry-mix method, while shotcrete covers both dry and wet mixes. The choice depends on the area, access and required strength.

What does guniting cost per square foot?

Cost depends on thickness, surface condition, scaffold height, mesh and material specification, so we price after a site inspection rather than quoting a flat rate. Send photos and the project location through the enquiry form and we will arrange a visit.

When does a building need structural repair?

Visible cracks, spalling concrete, exposed or rusting reinforcement, water seepage, deflection or vibration are reasons to have the structure inspected. Where a structural consultant's report exists, it is the right basis for the repair scope.

How long does concrete rehabilitation last?

Service life depends on the cause of deterioration, the specification, workmanship, cover and ongoing maintenance. A correctly prepared and cured repair with treated reinforcement and a protective finish lasts far longer than a cosmetic patch; expected maintenance is set out in the method statement.
