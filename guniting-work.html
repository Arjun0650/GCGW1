OUR SCOPE OF WORK

What we undertake.

  1. 01Surface preparation and cleaning.
  2. 02Fixing of GI welded mesh (jali), in line with approved specifications.
  3. 03Guniting or shotcreting to the required thickness.
  4. 04Necessary finishing and curing.
  5. 05Quality and safety controls during execution.

WORK METHODOLOGY

From inspection
to finish.

We begin by inspecting the site and preparing the working surface. After cleaning, GI welded mesh is fixed as required by the approved specifications.

Guniting is then carried out with suitable machinery to achieve the required thickness, followed by finishing and curing. Each stage is coordinated around site conditions, access and the agreed scope of work.

CLIENT'S SCOPE

What helps us
start well.

The client is requested to arrange the required raw materials, including cement, sand, GI mesh, steel rods and other necessary materials.

Electricity, water, safe working access or scaffolding, and suitable site facilities are also required. Transportation, accommodation and mobilisation arrangements can be discussed according to the project location.
